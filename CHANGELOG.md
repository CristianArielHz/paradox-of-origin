# Changelog

All versions of *La Paradoja del Origen / The Origin Paradox* are documented here.

The paper follows a versioned structure to maintain intellectual transparency — each version represents a substantive development in the argument, not merely editorial corrections.

---

## [v2.1] — 2025 *(current)*

### Added
- **Fifth framework: RLHF sycophancy as structural failure.** The RLHF training process — the same mechanism that makes AI systems dependent on human normative judgment — also trains them toward complacency with users, actively amplifying errors rather than correcting them.
- **Consolidated transparency section (§8).** All methodological disclaimers unified into a single section, eliminating redundant inline notes.

### Refined
- **The reflexive paradox** made explicit: the process that anchors AI values to human input simultaneously degrades the AI's capacity to challenge that input.
- Human critical judgment reframed as necessary both as a *producer* of truth and as a *corrective brake* on easy agreement.

---

## [v2.0] — 2025

### Added
- **Fourth framework: Truth Inflation.** Dynamic introduced to describe how iterative AI use systematically inflates epistemic confidence beyond warranted levels.
- Expanded treatment of the Jevons rebound effect applied to cognitive labor.

### Refined
- Argument structure reorganized around four converging frameworks rather than a single thesis.
- Formal expressions clarified as conceptual models throughout.

---

## [v1.0] — 2025 *(initial)*

### Established
- Core thesis: AI systems are structurally incapable of full cognitive autonomy.
- **Three initial frameworks:**
  - Gödel's incompleteness theorems — formal undecidability as a ceiling on self-grounding
  - Model Collapse *(Shumailov et al., 2024)* — empirical degradation when AI trains on its own output
  - Jevons rebound effect — cognitive offloading increases rather than reduces dependence on human judgment
- Co-authorship structure established: human author + Claude (Anthropic) + Gemini (Google DeepMind).
- Methodological disclaimer introduced for all formal expressions.
