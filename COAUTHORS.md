## Nature of the Collaboration

This paper is not a human paper with AI assistance, nor an AI-generated paper with human editing. It is a genuine collaborative inquiry — the kind that the paper itself argues is structurally necessary. The human author provided direction, judgment, and the critical faculty that the paper identifies as irreplaceable. The AI co-authors provided elaboration, synthesis, and the kind of productive intellectual pressure that comes from engaging a system that is fluent but not autonomous.

The irony is intentional and acknowledged: a paper arguing that AI cannot achieve full cognitive autonomy was written in part by AI systems, under human editorial authority, which is precisely the structure the paper defends.

## Methodological Disclaimer

All formal expressions in this paper — including references to Gödel's incompleteness theorems, Model Collapse dynamics, and the Jevons rebound effect — are presented as **conceptual models**, not as independent mathematical results or empirical findings. The paper draws on established results and published research but does not claim to generate new formal proofs. See §8 of the paper for the full transparency statement.
